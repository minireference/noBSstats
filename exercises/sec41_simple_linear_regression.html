Repeat the calculations to obtain the regression result object lm1 for the linear model \(S|e \sim \mathcal{N}(\beta_0 + \beta_1 e, \sigma)\), which describes the students’ scores \(S\) as a function of their effort \(e\).

a) Use the method lm1.get_prediction to predict the mean score for a student that invests \(10\) hours of \(\texttt{effort}\), \(\mu_{\widehat{S}}(10)\).

b) Construct a 90% confidence interval \(\ci{\mu_{S},0.9}(10)\) for the mean score \(\mu_{S}(10)\) by calling the .conf_int method.

c) Use lm1.get_prediction to predict the score of a student that invests \(10\) hours of \(\texttt{effort}\), \(\hat{s}(10)\).

d) Report a 90% prediction interval \(\ci{S,0.9}(10)\) for the score.
